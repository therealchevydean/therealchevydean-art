Built From The Ruins

The real beginning came in 2023, when Joshua started drawing while FaceTiming with his daughter. It was the only way to stay connected through distance. Those sketches became a lifeline — a way to keep hope alive, keep his mind steady, and keep showing up as a father when life was at its hardest.

His work did not come from art school or fancy studios. It came from survival. From homelessness. From rebuilding after incarceration. From sitting at kitchen tables and borrowed desks, turning pain into something worth sharing.

The drawings, the books, the systems, the worlds he builds now are more than art. They are pieces of a life rebuilt from scratch.

This is what survival looks like when it decides to build something.
